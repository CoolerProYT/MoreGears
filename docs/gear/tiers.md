# Tiers

More Gears has seven tiers. Each one has its own material, and most have a special effect. This page lists every item in each tier. For numbers, see [Tools & weapons](./tools), [Armor](./armor) and [Bows & arrows](./ranged).

<TierLadder />

| Tier | Mines like | Tools & weapons | Armor | Ranged | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Copper | – | Vanilla | Vanilla | Bow, crossbow, arrows | Lightning immunity (full set) |
| Bronze | Iron, plus ruby ore | ✔ + spear, shield | ✔ | ✔ | Fast mining underwater |
| Steel | Diamond | ✔ + spear, shield | ✔ | ✔ | Fast attacks, heavy to hold |
| Ruby | Diamond | ✔ + spear, shield | ✔ | ✔ | – |
| Echoite | Diamond | ✔ + spear, shield | ✔ | ✔ | Darkness wears off twice as fast |
| Titanium | Everything | ✔ + spear, shield, mace | ✔ | ✔ | Fireproof armor, piglins stay calm |
| Enderite | Everything | ✔ + spear, shield, mace, trident | ✔ | ✔ | Unbreakable, fire, fall and drowning immunity |

Every tier from bronze up also has a **shield**, and every tier with a bow has a **crossbow** that takes the same arrows. Enderite adds a **trident** and an **elytra**.

## Copper {#copper .tier .tier-copper}

<TierShowcase tier="copper" :extra="['minecraft:copper_sword', 'minecraft:copper_pickaxe', 'minecraft:copper_helmet', 'minecraft:copper_chestplate', 'minecraft:copper_leggings', 'minecraft:copper_boots']" />

Minecraft has its own copper tools and armor now, so More Gears only adds a **copper bow** and **copper arrows**. It also gives vanilla copper armor a perk: wear the full set and lightning can't hurt you.

## Bronze {#bronze .tier .tier-bronze}

<TierShowcase tier="bronze" />

An early upgrade over iron, made from copper and tin in the [Alloy Smelter](/guide/alloy-smelter). Bronze pickaxes, axes, shovels and hoes **mine three times faster while you're in water** and glow while they do. A bronze pickaxe is the first one that can mine [ruby ore](/guide/ores#ruby).

## Steel {#steel .tier .tier-steel}

<TierShowcase tier="steel" />

Iron alloyed with coal. Steel swords and tools **attack a full point per second faster** than their bronze and ruby versions, but holding one in either hand **slows you down**. Steel mines everything diamond can.

## Ruby {#ruby .tier .tier-ruby}

<TierShowcase tier="ruby" />

Mined from [ruby ore](/guide/ores#ruby). Ruby gear is a little weaker than diamond but mines the same blocks, and you can get it before you find diamonds.

## Echoite <Badge type="tip" text="26.3+" /> {#echoite .tier .tier-echoite}

<TierShowcase tier="echoite" />

The Deep Dark tier, sitting between ruby and titanium. Echoite ingots are made in the [Alloy Smelter](/guide/alloy-smelter#echoite) from an **echo shard and a diamond**, so you need to raid an ancient city before you can make any. It mines everything diamond can, and wearing the **full armor set makes the Darkness effect wear off twice as fast**, which takes a lot of the sting out of sculk shriekers and the warden.

## Titanium {#titanium .tier .tier-titanium}

<TierShowcase tier="titanium" />

Made by upgrading netherite gear with a [Titanium Upgrade](/guide/smithing-templates#titanium-upgrade). Titanium armor, spears and maces **don't burn** in fire or lava, and **piglins stay neutral** while you wear titanium armor. The **titanium mace** never breaks. A titanium pickaxe is the first one that can mine [enderite ore](/guide/ores#enderite).

## Enderite {#enderite .tier .tier-enderite}

<TierShowcase tier="enderite" />

The top tier, made by upgrading titanium gear with an [Enderite Upgrade](/guide/smithing-templates#enderite-upgrade). **Every enderite tool, weapon and armor piece is unbreakable and fireproof.** Enderite armor makes you immune to fire and lava, keeps piglins neutral, the boots stop fall damage, and the helmet stops drowning. See [Special effects](./special-effects#enderite) for everything it does.

Enderite is also the only tier with a **trident** and an **elytra**. Both are upgraded from their vanilla versions with the same template, and both are unbreakable and fireproof. The trident keeps Loyalty, Channeling and Riptide, and the elytra flies exactly like a vanilla one.
