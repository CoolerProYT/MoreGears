# Special effects

Most tiers do something beyond their stats. The item tooltip lists the effect in grey.

## Copper {.tier .tier-copper}

<div class="mg-effects">
  <div class="mg-effect tier-copper">
    <strong>Lightning immunity</strong>
    Wear a full set of <b>vanilla</b> copper armor and lightning can't hurt you. All four pieces are needed.
  </div>
</div>

## Bronze {.tier .tier-bronze}

<div class="mg-effects">
  <div class="mg-effect tier-bronze">
    <strong>Fast mining underwater</strong>
    While you're in water, the bronze pickaxe, axe, shovel or hoe in your main hand glows and mines <b>3× faster</b>.
  </div>
</div>

Underwater mining is normally five times slower, so bronze gets you most of the way back to normal speed. With Aqua Affinity on your helmet, a bronze tool mines faster underwater than it does on land. The sword, spear and armor don't have this effect.

## Steel {.tier .tier-steel}

<div class="mg-effects">
  <div class="mg-effect tier-steel">
    <strong>Faster attacks</strong>
    Steel swords and tools swing one attack per second faster than bronze or ruby ones.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-steel">
    <strong>Heavy</strong>
    Holding a steel sword or tool in either hand makes you walk about 20% slower. Holding two doesn't slow you more.
  </div>
</div>

The steel spear and armor have neither effect.

## Titanium {.tier .tier-titanium}

<div class="mg-effects">
  <div class="mg-effect tier-titanium">
    <strong>Fireproof</strong>
    Titanium armor, spears and maces survive fire and lava when dropped, like netherite.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-titanium">
    <strong>Piglins stay neutral</strong>
    Wearing any piece of titanium armor counts as wearing gold.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-titanium">
    <strong>Unbreakable mace</strong>
    The titanium mace never loses durability.
  </div>
</div>

Titanium swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes and bows are **not** fireproof, so keep them away from lava.

## Enderite {.tier .tier-enderite}

<div class="mg-effects">
  <div class="mg-effect tier-enderite">
    <strong>Unbreakable</strong>
    Every enderite tool, weapon and armor piece, including the bow, never loses durability.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-enderite">
    <strong>Fireproof</strong>
    Enderite gear survives fire and lava when dropped.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-enderite">
    <strong>Fire immunity</strong>
    Wearing any enderite armor piece blocks all fire, burning and lava damage, and puts you out when you catch fire.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-enderite">
    <strong>Boots: safe landings</strong>
    No fall damage, and magma blocks don't hurt.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-enderite">
    <strong>Helmet: water breathing</strong>
    You never drown. Crashing into a wall while gliding with an elytra doesn't hurt either.
  </div>
  <div class="mg-effect tier-enderite">
    <strong>Piglins stay neutral</strong>
    Wearing any piece of enderite armor counts as wearing gold.
  </div>
</div>
